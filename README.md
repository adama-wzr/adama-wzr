# Research Focus

My primary research interests include:
- Continuum-scale electrochemical modeling
- Thermal-fluid sciences
- Porous media characterization
- Decision-making based on large multimodal datasets

My work is driven by the challenge of linking detailed physical behavior with first principles continuum-scale modeling. I am especially interested in how transport phenomena and material structure influence system performance, and how large, multimodal datasets can support better scientific and engineering understanding.
